I am a fourth-year PhD student in **Economics** at **Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT)**. I hold a bachelor’s degree in Industrial Engineering and Management, with distinction, also from KIT. In Spring 2026, I am a visiting student in the Department of Economics at the University of California, **Berkeley**.

My research interests are in **macroeconomics** and **computational economics**, with a particular focus on heterogeneous-agent models, public finance, and numerical methods for dynamic economic models. You can find an overview of my research [here](al-folio/research) and my CV [here](al-folio/cv).
